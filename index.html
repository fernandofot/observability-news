How it works

A small, transparent pipeline — everything it does is public in the repository.

Official sources only

Every item comes from an official RSS or Atom feed: project blogs and GitHub release feeds. Nothing is scraped and nothing is promotional.

Collected every morning

An automated pipeline runs daily at 08:00 UTC, deduplicates items and applies editorial filters before anything enters the registry.

A public registry

Every item is tracked in a public JSON registry with its source, publication date and status — so you can audit exactly why something was included or left out.

What we cover today

Coverage starts small and honest: every item comes from one of four official feeds across two ecosystems.

The email digest is coming later

Subscriptions haven't opened yet. The daily collection pipeline is already running; email delivery is the next milestone. Until then, this site collects nothing — no email addresses, no tracking, no sign-up.
