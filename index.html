How it works

A small automated pipeline that collects official announcements and releases every day.

Official sources only

Every item comes from an official RSS or Atom feed: project blogs and GitHub release feeds. Nothing is scraped and nothing is promotional.

Collected daily

An automated pipeline is scheduled daily at 08:00 and 10:00 UTC, deduplicates items and applies editorial filters before anything is published. Actual publication times may vary.

Every item linked

Each entry links straight to the official source, so you can always read the original announcement or release notes.

What we cover today

Coverage starts small and honest: every item comes from one of six official feeds across three ecosystems.
